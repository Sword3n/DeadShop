## Notes

- Stats are base values at level 1, before upgrades, talents and item modifiers.
- `Sprint Mult` is a multiplier on base Move Speed.
- `Role` — Tank / Initiator / Gun carry / Spirit carry / Assassin / Support / Disruptor,
  per the Deadlock Handbook main-role taxonomy (every hero note records `role_source`).
- `Released` — hero launch date. The six *City Never Sleeps* heroes (Rat King, Solomon,
  Nurse Harrow, Baba, Deadman Danny, Violet) launched Oct 2026.
